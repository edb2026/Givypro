♻️ What We Do

We operate a circular system:

  • Collect unwanted items
  • Clean, repair, and restore what we can
  • Resell usable goods
  • Reduce landfill waste
  • Return part of the value back to users
📦 What We Accept

We help you easily remove unwanted household items — furniture, décor, office items, and more — so you can focus on what matters.

We accept items in:

  • New
  • Like New
  • Very Good (gently used)
  • Lightly Used (minor wear)

We also accept items with minor damage, as long as they are still usable.

💚 A Cycle of Positive Impact

It's more than just giving — it's a cycle of positive impact.

We use proceeds to keep pickups free, restore and reuse items, provide customer rewards, support community initiatives, and reduce environmental impact.

🌍 Why It Matters

Terms & Conditions


Item Acceptance & Condition Policy

We accept items in the following conditions: New, Like New, and, in some cases, Very Good Condition.

Items in New or Like New condition may still require minor repairs or partial cleaning.

Items classified as Very Good Condition may also be accepted; however, each item is reviewed individually, and acceptance is determined at our sole discretion.

By submitting or listing an item on our platform, you acknowledge and agree that we are not obligated to pick up or accept any item.

Rewards Points & Levels Policy

Points are awarded based on the type, size, and value of the items provided.

All point allocations are determined at our discretion.
